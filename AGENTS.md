# Bound Souls project rules

## Art direction

- All new and revised artwork must be high-quality 2D art with a consistent visual style across the game.
- Before creating assets, inspect existing approved artwork and user-provided references. Match rendering style, proportions, palette, shading, outline treatment, detail level, and animation quality so assets feel part of the same game.
- Do not default to pixel art merely because a sprite tool is available. Canvas dimensions do not determine the art style. If existing artwork and a new reference differ, clarify the intended direction before producing a mismatched asset.
- Do not substitute crude procedural drawings, placeholders, or lower-quality approximations for finished artwork. If available tools cannot achieve the requested quality or style, explain the limitation before proceeding.
- Present exact artwork and animation candidates for human review before importing or placing them in gameplay. Tool success is not visual approval.

## Soul animation direction

- Match Whisp's detailed pixel-art style for souls. Use Pokémon-style creature battles as inspiration for readable poses and timing, never as a source of copied sprites or effects.
- All soul idle animations should include a gentle, rhythmic up/down bob, typically 1–2 pixels on a 96×96 canvas. Preserve the character's silhouette, glow, and established personality; do not add head turns or exaggerated limb motion by default.
- Keep the canvas and pivot fixed across frames. Author the bob deliberately in the artwork rather than changing pivots or accidentally recentering frames. Check the loop transition for jumps and unwanted foot sliding.
- Attacks should have readable anticipation, action, and recovery, with restrained environmental effects suited to the soul (such as Moss's spores and green glow). Effects should support, not obscure, the character.
- Review motion playback as well as contact sheets. State when playback has not been watched; successful generation is not proof of animation quality.
- Apply this direction to new or explicitly requested revisions; do not silently replace other souls' approved artwork or gameplay assets.
